# Journi Experience Intelligence Knowledge Base
This document is the curated domain intelligence knowledge base for the Journi travel platform. It contains permanent curated experiences across Indian cultural hubs and seasonal cultural festival trails.

---

## 1. Domain Reasoning Guidelines for Journi AI Concierge
- **Pacing & Rest Intervals:** Pair high-energy stops (bazaars, walking tours) with restful spots (cafes, stepwells, parks).
- **Time Constraints:** Respect operating hours, opening times, and typical duration.
- **Budget Sensitivity:** Balance free/budget street experiences with premium heritage visits.
- **Weather Adaptation:** Recommend indoor/sheltered experiences during midday heat or monsoon rains; prioritize open-air bazaars and heritage trails during pleasant morning or evening hours.
- **Cultural Authenticity:** Prioritize multi-generational heritage workshops, verified local artisans, and genuine community traditions.
